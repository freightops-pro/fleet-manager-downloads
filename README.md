# City of Groves Fleet Manager — Downloads

Download the Fleet Manager app for the City of Groves municipal garage.

**Latest version: 1.3.0** — [go to the download page](https://github.com/freightops-pro/fleet-manager-downloads/releases/latest)

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Windows PC (shop terminals, office) | `FleetManager-1.3.0-windows.zip` |
| Android phone or tablet | `FleetManager-1.3.0-android.apk` |

## Install on Windows

1. Download `FleetManager-1.3.0-windows.zip`.
2. Right-click the zip and choose **Extract All…**. Pick a permanent place, such as `C:\FleetManager`.
3. Open the extracted folder and double-click **fleet_manager.exe**.
4. The first time, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
   This appears because the app is not code-signed yet.

Keep every file in the folder together. The app will not start if `fleet_manager.exe` is moved out on its own.

## Install on Android

1. Download `FleetManager-1.3.0-android.apk` on the phone or tablet.
2. Open it. If Android asks, allow your browser or file app to **install unknown apps**.
3. Tap **Install**, then open **Fleet Manager**.

## Signing in

Sign in with the username and password your shop manager gave you.
Forgotten passwords are reset by a shop manager under **Shop Manager → Users**.

Tick **Remember me on this device** and choose a 4–6 digit PIN. After that you only enter
your PIN (or use Face ID / fingerprint on a phone). Five wrong PINs, a password reset, or
**Sign out & forget this device** sends you back to the password screen.

Department heads sign in the same way and see only their own department's vehicle costs.

## What's new in 1.3.0

- **Cost tracking.** Every vehicle's labor, parts, fluids, outside-vendor and other expenses,
  with cost per mile.
- **Price history.** Every part keeps its past prices, with the change since the last price.
  Bulk items (drums, totes) can be entered by container price; the unit cost is worked out.
- **Lubricants and additives.** Log oil, DEF, antifreeze and the like to a vehicle or charge
  them to a department, in quarts or gallons. Stock is deducted automatically. The shop's
  lubricant price list is preloaded.
- **Mileage log.** Odometer readings are kept for every vehicle.
- **Expenses.** Fuel, tires, towing, registration, insurance and other costs per vehicle.
- **Cost reports.** By fiscal year (Oct 1 – Sep 30), quarter, month, week, day or any date
  range, with pie charts, trends, this year against last year, and CSV exports.
- **Department heads** get a read-only view of their own department's vehicle costs.
- **Remember me** with a PIN, plus Face ID / fingerprint on phones.

## What's new in 1.2.1
